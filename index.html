Privacy Policy for Poor Bird

Flarix Studios built the Poor Bird game as a commercial app. This service is provided by Flarix Studios and is intended for use as is.

Information Collection and Use

For a better experience while playing, our app integrates third-party advertising services (Google AdMob) which may collect and process user data. This data is handled strictly in accordance with Google Play's developer requirements and includes:

  • Device or Other Identifiers: Advertising IDs used by AdMob to serve and personalize ads.
  • Location Data: Approximate location derived from IP addresses to provide regionally relevant ads.
  • App Activity & Interactions: Analytics tracking ad views, clicks, and basic app engagement.
  • Diagnostics: App performance data and crash logs to monitor game stability.

All data transmitted to third-party ad networks is encrypted in transit. For more details, you can review the official Google Partner Privacy Policy.
